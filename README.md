A simple datapack that adds a copy of the spears from the snapshot 25w42a to older releases of the game.

### Includes:
Accurate item models and accurate weapon attributes. Charge ability: when holding right click deal damage to entities 2-4.5 blocks infront of you based on your current velocity. Lunge enchantment: obtained through the enchanting table or trading with villagers, upon right click dash forward an amount and lose durability based on level.

### Supported versions:
1.21.9-1.21.10
